Our Story

Born from a Love of Good Coffee & Good Company

Brewed Awakening started as a single window shop in Anna Nagar, where we served filter coffee the way it was meant to be — slow-dripped, perfectly sweetened, served in a stainless steel tumbler with care.

Today we've grown into a full café, but the soul remains the same. We source single-origin beans from Coorg and Chikmagalur, pair them with imported Italian espresso equipment, and offer a menu that feels as comfortable to a chai lover as it does to a flat white enthusiast.

Every visit should feel like home. That's our only promise.
